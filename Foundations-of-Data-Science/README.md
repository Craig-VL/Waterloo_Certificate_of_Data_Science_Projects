**Group Project for Foundations of Data Science Course** \
*Author: Craig Vande Ligt*

**Topic:** Industrial energy use and emission trends in Ontario's broad public sector (2011 through 2023) \
**Objective:** To determine whether emissions from industrial public operation sector buildings
are more volatile over time compared to other public operation sectors, and higher in volume.
Analysis will be done to determine key differences over time between operation sectors. \
**Dataset:** https://data.ontario.ca/dataset/energy-use-and-greenhouse-gas-emissions-for-the-broader-public-sector \
**Summary:**
- Analyzed BPS industrial energy use and GHG emission trends using annual government datasets
- Built end-to-end analytics workflow, including web scraping, harmonizing schema differences across years cleaning and imputing missing data, engineering emissions intensity variables, and joining postal-code geocoordinates to support geographic slicing and aggregation
- Performed sector-based exploratory analysis and trend assessment (delta analysis), and fit a linear regression model to quantify emissions patterns over time and differences across sectors; found overall decline in BPS emissions aligned with progress toward reduction targets, and identified important data quality limitations to guide interpretation
